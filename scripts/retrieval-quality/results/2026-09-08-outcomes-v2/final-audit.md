## Findings and limitations

1. **Navigation instrument limitation, preserved rather than repaired retrospectively.** The frozen rubric requires an explicit no-double-counting cache warning; the prompt asks for counters and broad interpretation boundaries without expressly requesting that warning. The six answers calculate the counters correctly but omit the required warning. Their strict failures remain valid under that rubric, while the mismatch limits any broader conclusion about answer quality. The report discloses this and suppresses navigation deltas. A changed criterion requires a future separately frozen experiment.
2. **Protected assignment text limits delivery verification.** Prepared inputs and spawn metadata verify as described in E6. The protected runtime strings cannot establish byte-equality with delivered messages. No mismatch is asserted, and no exact-delivery certification is earned.
3. **Source, host, model and overhead measurements remain distinct.** Receipt joins validate the thirteen aide operations; manual source-return decoding and outer truncation have separate evidentiary boundaries. Cached/reasoning subsets are not added again. The overhead snapshot deliberately omits responses completing after its cutoff, including some of this audit. Zero-uptake code comparisons cannot establish a retrieval-tool benefit.

No new production collection or recovery work is included. No grade, frozen input or participant evidence was altered by this audit. Approval applies to retaining and presenting this small diagnostic experiment with its stated limits.
