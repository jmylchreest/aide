3. **Three writer attempts, two stored variants.** Broadcast 1 attempts and fails without entering the cache; broadcast 2 retries, succeeds and is cached; broadcast 3 is suppressed. Broadcast 4 changes the normalized event (`uncached_input_tokens=12`, `input_tokens=32`), so it attempts and succeeds. The recorder’s bounded, per-instance cache keys `JSON.stringify([cwd,next])` and remembers only acknowledged writes. Durable deduplication uses logical identity plus a fingerprint containing counters and metadata, so equal retries deduplicate across cache loss, while changed counters survive as a distinct variant. Equal variants retain the earliest timestamp. Evidence: `src/core/model-usage.ts:349–362`; `aide/pkg/store/token_model_usage.go:46–68`; `aide/pkg/store/observe_events.go:166–216`.

4. **The identity becomes one conflict and contributes no counters.** Reporting groups both variants under the same logical identity and marks their differing fingerprints as conflicting. For this identity alone, model usage reports `conflicts=1`, `observations=0`, `invalid=0`, and no source group; it does not choose the latest variant or sum them. A date filter selecting only the earlier variant still reports the conflict and cannot restore its counters: all stored observations are examined before date filtering, and any selected variant makes the conflicting identity count once. Evidence: `aide/pkg/store/token_events.go:153–163`, `aide/pkg/store/token_events.go:191–196`; `aide/pkg/store/token_model_usage.go:150–163`, `aide/pkg/store/token_model_usage.go:172–205`.

Missing counters remain distinct from reported zero, and sources must remain separate.
