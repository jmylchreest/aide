# jmylchreest-writing-change-notes

**Decision:** Explain the concrete change and its reason in commit messages and change notes.

## Rationale

Useful change history lets a future reader understand what happened and why without replaying the development session.

## Details

Use a short, informative commit subject that follows the repository convention. Add complete sentences in the body when the reason, resulting behaviour, validation or a limitation needs explanation. Describe the final change rather than the chronology of edits. Mention checks only when they were actually run. Avoid generic claims such as "improve robustness" without explaining the improvement.
