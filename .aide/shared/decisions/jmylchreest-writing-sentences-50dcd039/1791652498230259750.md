# jmylchreest-writing-sentences

**Decision:** Use complete, connected sentences and avoid em dashes in authored prose.

## Rationale

Complete sentences make relationships between ideas clear without forcing the reader to reconstruct the meaning.

## Details

Do not use em dashes, clipped sentence fragments or artificial question-and-answer constructions. Use a full stop, comma, colon or parentheses as the sentence requires. Connect related ideas without creating long, tangled sentences. Keep headings, labels, identifiers and conventional commit subjects brief where their format calls for it. Preserve exact quotations, commands and code syntax.
