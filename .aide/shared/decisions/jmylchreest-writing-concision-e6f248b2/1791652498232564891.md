# jmylchreest-writing-concision

**Decision:** Keep writing succinct while preserving the meaning and context the reader needs.

## Rationale

Concise writing saves attention when it removes repetition without hiding useful information.

## Details

Prefer specific verbs and concrete examples to vague qualifiers. Remove filler, repeated summaries and unnecessary background. Give each paragraph one clear purpose. Use lists when they make steps or parallel items easier to scan. Do not shorten explanations into fragments or omit relevant limitations just to reduce word count.
