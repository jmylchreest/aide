# survey-store-location

**Decision:** Separate BoltDB+Bleve store at .aide/survey/ directory

## Rationale

Survey entries are structural codebase facts — different from memories (temporal), decisions (ADRs), or findings (code problems). Separate store follows the findings pattern and keeps domain boundaries clean.
